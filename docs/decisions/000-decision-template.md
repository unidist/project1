# 000 — Decision record template

Copy this file, number it in sequence, name it after the decision:
`001-hosting-platform.md`, `002-command-prefix.md`.

Keep each one short. If it takes more than a page you're writing an essay, not a
record. The purpose is that in three months someone can read it in ninety seconds
and stop wondering.

Never edit a record after it's merged. If you change your mind, write a new one
that says it supersedes the old — the reasoning you rejected is often more useful
later than the reasoning you kept.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [00X]

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Decision

What we're doing, in one or two sentences. Present tense, no hedging.

## Context

What made this a question. What constraints were in play — time, money, what
people already know, what we already built.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and specifically why it lost. This is the section
people actually come back for. "We considered X" is useless; "we considered X but
it required everyone to learn Y in week one" is the record.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, and what it makes harder or forecloses. Every real
decision costs something — if you can't name the cost, you probably haven't found
the tradeoff yet.

## Who decided

Names, and roughly how. Consensus, or one person called it.
